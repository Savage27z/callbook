In plain English

A report card for people
who make predictions.

Everyone has a friend, a pundit or a website that sounds sure of itself. CallBook keeps score. You write down what each one predicted before an event, enter what really happened, and the app shows who has earned your trust.

How it works

Three steps. No more guessing
who to trust.

1

Lock in a prediction

Before the event, write down what each forecaster said, like “70% the Ravens win”. It is saved on the Sui blockchain with a timestamp, so nobody can change their answer afterwards.

2

Enter the result

When the event is over, tell CallBook what happened. Each forecaster is scored: right and confident scores well, confident and wrong scores badly.

3

See who to trust

Every score is saved in Walrus. CallBook then shows an average that listens more to forecasters with the best record, and any AI tool that supports MCP can read the same record.
